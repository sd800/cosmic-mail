A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Simply answered.

Which files can I open?

Open .eml/.mime/.emlx/.msg/.oft/.mbox/.mbx/.mbo files, Apple Mail mailbox folders, and Maildir folders. MBX files use the MBOX format.

For Outlook PST/OST/OLM archives, export to EML or MBOX first.

Does it replace my email app?

Cosmic Mail is a reader for local email and a deliberate way to handle email links. Keep using your existing email service for your inbox, syncing, and sending.

How do I catch email links?

Open Cosmic Mail → Settings ⌘, and choose Use Cosmic Mail for email links. It’s your choice: installing the app doesn’t change your default apps.

How do I install Cosmic Mail?

Download the latest app, unzip it, and move Cosmic Mail.app to Applications. The universal app supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

The app is ad hoc signed. If macOS asks you to approve the first launch, check that the download is from sd800/cosmic-mail, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
